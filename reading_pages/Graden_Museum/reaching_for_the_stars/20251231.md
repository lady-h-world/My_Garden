## 2025 Last Day
Today is the lats day of 2025. To me, there's nothing special, but to take a real good rest.

I was very glad that yesterday Google approved my app and now it's published in the Play Store. In order to be publised, Google requires at least 12 testers to test the app in 14 continuous days, but it didn't have clear testing requirements. I checked Reddit, Gemini, ChatGPT, got very confusing answers. So I had to use the most strict testing approach to live through these 14 days. These testers are friends, friends' friends, colleagues, colleagues' friends, except Christmas time, I was pushing them to interact with the app's features almost everyday. They're very nice, most of them gave me very good feedbacks, even found a critical issue and a Samsumg specific blocker. In order to echo back their kindness, I tried my best to prioritize the fixes and improvements, got most important ones done and did the new releases, hoping by doing this they can feel being heard. At the same time, I was looking for a living place in San Francisco and full time jobs there.

Frist of all, I can't all in to do startup, not because of financial pressure, but because that type of rhythm and stress doesn't fit me. I can only work on products in leisure time, and let it be, when the time matures, it might become a company. My happiness and health comes first, and I really want to cherish the time when I am still able to take parents travel abroad.
